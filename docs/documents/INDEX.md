- [Site project](site-project.md) — the Astro site in `site/`: stack, commands, file layout, Data Card markup, pages and routes over the persisted World (the Garden's Project list and Project pages, the Dock's Client and Partner lists, About and Team opening the House, Contact's details with no form, camera flights, Back and Forward), the full-screen layout (floating cards, the free area the camera frames Landmarks in, the scrolling fallback without JS or a GPU, with the build-time World still captured by Playwright in Day and Night, icon-only controls on phones), the Jersey fonts and contrast, Day and Night (the OKLCH theme tokens shared with the World, the Auto, Day and Night toggle, no flash on load), exploring the World, Seed in the URL and the dice button, the Guide (its name, its lines in the `pages` collection, when it says them, its button and speech bubble), the Tour (its button, its steps in Story Order with lines per Landmark, the Tour's card, next, previous and exit, the keyboard, what ends it), phones and the render tier (`?tier=`), reduced motion, the render stats overlay for developers (dev or `?stats`), and SEO and link previews (canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter tags, the OG image cut from the World still, the sitemap and `robots.txt`).
