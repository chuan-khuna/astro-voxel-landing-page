# Documents

One bullet per document in `docs/documents/`: `- Title — one-line summary`.

- [Site project](site-project.md) — the Astro site in `site/`: stack, commands, file layout, Data Card markup, pages and routes over the persisted World (the Garden's Project list and Project pages, the Dock's Client and Partner lists, About and Team opening the House, Contact's details with no form, camera flights, Back and Forward), the full-screen layout (floating cards, the free area the camera frames Landmarks in, the scrolling fallback without JS or a GPU, with the build-time World still captured by Playwright in Day and Night, icon-only controls on phones), the Jersey fonts and contrast, Day and Night (the OKLCH theme tokens shared with the World, the Auto, Day and Night toggle, no flash on load), exploring the World, Seed in the URL and the dice button, the Guide (its name, its lines in the `pages` collection, when it says them, its button and speech bubble), the Tour (its button, its steps in Story Order with lines per Landmark, the Tour's card, next, previous and exit, the keyboard, what ends it), phones and the render tier (`?tier=`), reduced motion, the render stats overlay for developers (dev or `?stats`), and SEO and link previews (canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter tags, the OG image cut from the World still, the sitemap and `robots.txt`).
- [Content model](content-model.md) — where the site's words come from: the site data module (identity, site URL, SEO and OG defaults, Landmark list, Data Card metrics) and the content collections (`pages`, Projects, Services, Clients, Partners, Team) with their schemas, what reaches the World (counts and stable ids only), and which title, description and image each page's link preview takes.
- [World generator](world-generator.md) — `generateWorld(seed, contentCounts)`: interface, World data shape, terrain, Isles and their radii, bridges, paths, Landmark footprints (levelling, occlusion by terrain and by each other, the Garden's trees, the House's 4×4 plot with its door and the Mailbox in its yard), the Dock's moored boats, the Partners' sailing loops, the buoys, Biomes, the Seed's pick of Landmark Variants, retry and tests.
- [Rendering and shading](rendering-and-shading.md) — World to pixels: voxel grids, the mesher (culling, AO, greedy merge, chunks), the soft-voxel TSL material, voxel tiers and the three grids (terrain 1, ground detail ¼, structure ⅛), the ¼-unit Landmark parts left (the Garden, the Dock's deck), the House (its Variant as a structure, a liftable lid, lit window faces) and its room (the interior space, the developer at work, the code screen), boats and sailing ships (local-space material, smoothed loops), buoys, sea, the World Web Worker and cache, scene runtime (swapping Worlds, render on demand, frames while ships sail or the House is open), Day and Night (sky, moonlight, darker water, the House's lit windows, glowing buoys, never the layout), the render tier (no MSAA, a smaller shadow map and ambient frames at 30 fps on phones), camera pan and zoom with bounds, Landmark focus and camera flights, frame reports and voxel counts for the stats overlay, the Guide (its voxel model and pixel-eye faces, where it floats, assembling from flying voxels, dissolving and re-forming on a new Seed, idling, flying ahead on the Tour, its speech bubble's placement), Landmark hotspots, the developer's link, the Garden's tree links, Data Cards (which shows, the `matrix3d` tilt, anchors) and the triangle budget.
- [Voxel structures](voxel-structures.md) — predefined ASCII voxel models at ⅛ unit: the file format (header, key, layers, colour roles, the liftable lid part), parsing at build time with a Vite plugin, the registry, placing a structure in a World (turning to its door) and instancing repeated ones, the Landmark Variants (House, Station, Mailbox and Dock in each Biome's style), the data tests, and the dev-only Model Gallery at `/dev/models`.
