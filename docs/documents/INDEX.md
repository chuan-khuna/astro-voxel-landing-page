# Documents

One bullet per document in `docs/documents/`: `- Title — one-line summary`.

- [Site project](site-project.md) — the Astro site in `site/`: stack, commands, file layout, Data Card markup, pages and routes over the persisted World (the Garden's Project list and Project pages, the Dock's Client and Partner lists, About and Team opening the House, Contact's details with no form, camera flights, Back and Forward), the full-screen layout (floating cards, the free area the camera frames Landmarks in, the scrolling fallback without JS or a GPU, with the build-time World still captured by Playwright in Day and Night, icon-only controls on phones), the Jersey fonts and contrast, Day and Night (the OKLCH theme tokens shared with the World, the Auto, Day and Night toggle, no flash on load), exploring the World, Seed in the URL and the dice button, the Guide (its name, its lines in the `pages` collection, when it says them, its button and speech bubble), the Tour (its button, its steps in Story Order with lines per Landmark, the Tour's card, next, previous and exit, the keyboard, what ends it), phones (the Story Scroll: every section on one scrolling page over the World, the camera following the scroll from Landmark to Landmark, the URL following the section in view, tap targets), the render tier (`?tier=`) and the Ambient Life tier (`?life=`), reduced motion, the render stats overlay for developers (dev or `?stats`), the browser tests (`bun run e2e`: Playwright on the built site, desktop and phone), and SEO and link previews (canonical URLs, Open Graph and Twitter tags, the OG image cut from the World still, the sitemap and `robots.txt`).
- [Content model](content-model.md) — where the site's words come from: the site data module (identity, site URL, SEO and OG defaults, Landmark list, Data Card metrics) and the content collections (`pages`, Projects, Services, Clients, Partners, Team) with their schemas (the Pinned Project flag and Garden order), what reaches the World (counts, and the Clients' and Partners' ids for their boat and ship kinds), and which title, description and image each page's link preview takes.
- [World generator](world-generator.md) — `generateWorld(seed, contentCounts)`: interface, World data shape, terrain, Isles and their radii, bridges, paths, Landmark footprints (levelling, occlusion by terrain and by each other, the Garden's 5×5 bed of Pinned Project flowers and trees with its gate, the House's 4×4 plot with its door and the Mailbox in its yard), the Dock's moored boats, the Partners' sailing loops, boat and ship kinds from the Clients' and Partners' ids, the buoys, Decoration (placed per Biome below the nearest Landmark and out of every Landmark's sight line), the particles' spawn areas (per Biome, by Day and at Night, off every footprint and out of every sight line), the clouds (shaped from the Seed, drifting down the wind over the sea, never over an Isle nor seen over one), the fish schools (in the open shallows, clear of the jetty, boats and ships), Biomes, the Seed's pick of Landmark Variants, retry and tests.
- [Rendering and shading](rendering-and-shading.md) — World to pixels: voxel grids, the mesher (culling, AO, greedy merge, chunks), the soft-voxel TSL material, voxel tiers and the three grids (terrain 1, ground detail ¼, structure ⅛), the ¼-unit Landmark parts left (the Dock's deck), the Garden (its fence and instanced flowers and trees), the House (its Variant as a structure, a liftable lid, lit window faces) and its room (the House Interior at 1/16 unit in the World, shown only while the lid is up: the developer at work, the code screen, the lamp's warm light at Night), boats and sailing ships (their kinds as instanced structures, local-space material, smoothed loops), buoys, Decoration (instanced, half of it on the reduced tier), the sea (a 1-unit voxel layer: shallow to deep by distance from the coast, translucent over the seabed, bobbing in the shader), the World Web Worker and cache, scene runtime (swapping Worlds, the render loop: continuous while Ambient Life is alive, on demand otherwise), Ambient Life's loop, clock and tiers (seen and alive, one shared clock and Seeded wind with rolling gusts, full, reduced and still tiers that drop on slow frames and never climb back, still under reduced motion), Ambient Life's wind, leaves and swell (plants swaying in the vertex shader by at most half a voxel, ⅛-unit leaves falling down the wind from the trees, boats and ships rocking in step with the sea), Ambient Life's butterflies, fireflies, snow and ash (one instanced mesh of ⅛-unit cubes per kind, moved in the shader, the kinds out by Day or at Night, never clickable), Ambient Life's clouds and their shadows (1-unit voxel clouds drifting over the sea in one translucent mesh, fading out as the camera zooms in, dark at Night; soft shadows from a drifting noise mask in the voxel and sea materials, none at Night), Ambient Life's shore waves, fish and glowing surf (⅛-unit foam running in along the coast in the sea's shader, calm by the jetty and boats; schools of instanced fish swimming under the shallows and leaping with spray; a faint glow where waves break at Night), Day and Night (sky, moonlight, darker water, the House's lit windows, glowing buoys, never the layout), the render tier (no MSAA, a smaller shadow map, ambient frames at 30 fps and half the Decoration on phones), camera pan and zoom with bounds (and no input on the Story Scroll), Landmark focus and camera flights, the Story Scroll's camera (scroll position to framing, damping, the lid following the scroll), frame reports and voxel counts for the stats overlay, the Guide (its voxel model and pixel-eye faces, where it floats, assembling from flying voxels, dissolving and re-forming on a new Seed, idling, flying ahead on the Tour, its speech bubble's placement), Landmark hotspots, the developer's link, the Garden's plant links (sized so none covers another), Data Cards (which shows, the `matrix3d` tilt, anchors) and the triangle budget.
- [Voxel structures](voxel-structures.md) — predefined ASCII voxel models at ⅛ unit (1/16 for the House Interior): the file format (header, key, layers, colour roles, the liftable lid part), parsing at build time with a Vite plugin, the registry, placing a structure in a World (turning to its door) and instancing repeated ones, the Landmark Variants (House, Station, Mailbox, Dock and the Garden's fence in each Biome's style), the Garden's trees and flowers per Biome, the boat and ship kinds per Biome, the Decoration per Biome (grass, flower clumps, rocks, bushes, small trees, fences, lamps, sheds, windmills), the fish (one species per Biome, one voxel tall), the House Interior rooms at 1/16 unit (one per Biome, the seated developer, drawn in the World while the House is open), the data tests, and the dev-only Model Gallery at `/dev/models`.
