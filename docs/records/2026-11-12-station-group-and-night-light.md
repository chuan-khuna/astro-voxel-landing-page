# Station group and Night light

Date: 2026-11-12

## What was discussed

The open questions in polish tickets 15 (Station group with a structure per Service) and 16 (torches and Night light). They were asked one at a time, in Thai.

## What was decided

**Ticket 15: Station group**

- **Layout: a 6×6 ring.** The lighthouse is at the centre, with a Service on each side; the corners are empty.
- **More Services use the diagonals.** The first four Services take the sides and Services 5–8 the corners. At most 8 show in the World; the rest appear only on `/services`.
- **Services are not clickable.** The whole group is the Station and opens `/services`.
- **The centre is a lighthouse.** It replaces ticket 03's Station Variants (a hut with a lattice mast and a round tower), in keeping with the company name and the glossary.

**Ticket 16: Night light**

- **The House room is lit at Night** by the lamp, hearth or brazier it already has, as Light Sources. No new lamps or candles.
- **Moonlight dims a little** (about 20–30%), so torches stand out while Landmarks still read and Night contrast holds.

## Earlier the same day
