# Phone scroll camera

Date: 2026-11-12

## What was discussed

How the site should work on a phone. The user asked for the site to be responsive, for the camera on a phone to take the visitor to each place in turn, moving slowly, and for the phone never to zoom out to the whole Isle.

## What was decided

- **The camera follows the scroll on a phone.** The sections stack as one scrolling page over the World, and the camera glides from one Landmark to the next as the visitor scrolls. It does not move by itself (no autoplay) and does not wait for menu taps.
- **No Overview on a phone.** The camera stays at Landmark close-ups; the intro frames a Landmark too.
- **Desktop and tablet keep today's layout and camera.**
- The rest of the site gets a responsive pass at every width.
