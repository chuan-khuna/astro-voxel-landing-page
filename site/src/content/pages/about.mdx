Lighthouse Data is a small studio of data and AI engineers. We like quiet
systems: pipelines that do not page anyone at night, models that can explain
where their numbers came from, and reports people actually open.

We work in the open with your team, and we leave behind code you can run
without us. That is one of us at the desk inside: say hello.
