## The question

Forty years of scanned pages, and a search box that only matched exact
words. Reporters spent mornings in the basement instead.

## What we built

We cleaned the OCR, split pages into articles, and built a semantic index
that understands that "harbour expansion" and "new quay" are the same
story. A small evaluation set, written with the reporters, keeps the
ranking honest as the index grows.

## What changed

Archive lookups that took a morning now take a minute, and old stories
turn up in new reporting every week.
