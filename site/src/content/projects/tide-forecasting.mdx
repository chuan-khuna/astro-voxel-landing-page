## The question

The harbour master planned berths from a whiteboard and a hunch. Ships
arrived early, late, or all at once, and every surprise cost a pilot boat
and an afternoon.

## What we built

A nightly pipeline joins tide gauges, vessel tracking and the booking
system, and a forecasting model predicts berth demand for the next three
days. Every forecast is reproducible: the same inputs give the same answer,
and each run records exactly which data it saw.

## What changed

- Berth conflicts fell by about a third in the first season.
- The whiteboard is still there. It now shows the forecast.
