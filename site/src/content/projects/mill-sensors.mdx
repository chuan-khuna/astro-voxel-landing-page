## The question

The mill's machines are older than most of its staff. When a bearing
failed, the line stopped for two days.

## What we built

Cheap vibration sensors on every critical bearing, a streaming pipeline,
and an anomaly model trained on what "healthy" sounds like for each
machine. Alerts arrive with a plain explanation and a chart, not a score.

## What changed

Two failing bearings were replaced on a planned stop in the first year.
Neither line stopped unplanned.
