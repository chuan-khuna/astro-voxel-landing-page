## The question

Each evening a planner rebuilt the next day's timetable by hand, juggling
weather, crews and demand between five ports.

## What we built

A demand forecast per crossing and an optimiser that proposes a timetable
the planner can adjust. Every plan is reproducible from its inputs, so a
change is always explained.

## What changed

Planning takes twenty minutes instead of two hours, and fewer crossings run
nearly empty.
