## The question

Forty stores, three energy suppliers, and invoices in four formats. Nobody
could say which store used the most, or why.

## What we built

A small warehouse that loads every invoice and meter reading, tested
transformations that put them on one scale, and a dashboard with one
headline number per store and the reasons behind it.

## What changed

The chain found two stores with faulty refrigeration in the first month,
and now sets energy targets per store.
