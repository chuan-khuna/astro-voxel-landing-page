year: 2025
tags: [ml, healthcare, experimentation]
---

## The question

One appointment in six was a no-show, and the clinics could not tell which
one.

## What we built

A no-show model from appointment history, with features the clinic staff
could read and argue with, and a randomised trial of reminder messages to
find which ones actually helped.

## What changed

No-shows dropped to about one in ten, and the trial results now decide
which reminders go out.
